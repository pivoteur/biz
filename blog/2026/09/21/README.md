today](https://x.com/marcinbunsch/status/2101739348932927787):

The real question is why are YOU reviewing code? No façile answers: what is the

review will suffice.

The wetware.

Look, AI (LLM) has its limitations, but I've found that the intelligence of

Before griping about AI, you may want to take a good hard look into the

## AI's coming for meeeee!

"AI's going to take my job and replace it with somebody who uses AI and gets 
paid 50% of what I got paid!"

Yes, it will.

Except, were the jobs AI replaces of any value to begin with?

I got let go, because I was paid WAY too much to do what AI does now.

Wah. Boo-hoo.

Except here's the flip side of that story. I now use AI to build tools for my 
own company from IP I developed.

AI is my support-tool, and it's helping me to build a company 10x, no 100x, no 
10,000x the value of the job I got replaced out of.

A lot of people are worried that AI is going to replace them.

They are right.

But don't be one of those people.

Be one of those people who use AI to leap up out of your  job (that always has 
been useless).

Build something worthwhile WITH AI, something you're proud of.

Be me.
